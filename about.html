About Us

Guardian Gas Solutions

Guardian Gas Solutions LLC is a family-owned, Florida-licensed propane and natural gas contractor serving homes and businesses across Central Florida. We bring focused gas-system expertise to design, installation, service, and maintenance, with safety and efficiency guiding every project.

Calvin Geer

Owner & Master Qualifier | Guardian Gas Solutions LLC

Calvin Geer’s passion for understanding how things work began at a young age. A strong mechanical aptitude and a hands-on approach led him into the propane energy industry in his early twenties, where he built the experience that now guides Guardian Gas Solutions.

His focus is clear: thoughtful system design, quality workmanship, and dependable performance. Calvin approaches each project as a complete system—considering how fuel supply, piping, pressure, and appliance demand work together to serve the property safely and efficiently. He believes the best results begin with careful planning and carry through every connection, test, and final detail.

As Owner and Master Qualifier, Calvin brings technical judgment and personal accountability to the company’s work. He cares about the details customers can see—and the ones they rely on without ever noticing. For him, project quality means building gas systems that meet today’s needs, account for future demands, and deliver lasting value.

Safety and efficiency guide the design. Pride in the work carries it through.
