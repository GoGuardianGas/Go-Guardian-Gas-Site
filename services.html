01HomeownersComfort • convenience • backup power

Make gas work for the way you live.

Start with what you want to power. We help connect the appliance plan to the piping, propane tank or natural gas supply, and installation work your property needs.

What we can handle

  • Gas connections for ranges, dryers, tankless water heaters, fireplaces, pool heaters, and outdoor kitchens.
  • New or extended gas piping, propane tank installations and replacements, regulators, and generator fuel supply.
  • Existing-system evaluations, pressure testing, leak detection and repairs, and scheduled service.

How we coordinate

We review the proposed appliances and existing supply, discuss routing and access, and define the gas work, permit needs, and site preparation in a written scope.

What to send us

  1. Property address and whether you use propane, natural gas, or are unsure.
  2. Appliance brands/model numbers and photos of the tank or meter and installation area.
  3. Your preferred timing and any upcoming construction or appliance delivery.

Planning future appliances? Include them early so capacity and routing can be considered together.

02Generator Dealers & InstallersFuel supply • dealer coordination

You handle the generator. We handle its gas supply.

Bring GGS into the project before the fuel connection becomes the last unresolved item. We coordinate the gas portion with the dealer, electrician, property owner, and supply provider as needed.

What we can handle

  • Review of generator fuel demand, available gas supply, proposed route, and other connected appliances.
  • Dedicated gas piping, regulators, shutoffs, and propane tank installation or supply modifications within the agreed scope.
  • Gas permit preparation, pressure testing, inspection coordination, and gas-side completion documentation as applicable.

How we coordinate

Define the fuel connection location, site readiness, installation sequence, and responsibilities before dispatch. Electrical work, generator commissioning, and utility meter changes remain coordinated with the responsible providers.

What to send us

  1. Generator make/model, fuel type, installation address, and planned startup date.
  2. Site plan or photos showing the generator, existing tank or meter, and proposed route.
  3. Dealer contact, contracting party, and who handles electrical work and commissioning.

A larger generator may change the whole-property gas demand. Include the existing appliance list when requesting a quote.

03General Contractors & HomebuildersNew builds • additions • renovation

Put the gas scope on the construction schedule.

For new homes, additions, tenant improvements, and renovations, we help turn plans and appliance selections into a coordinated gas installation scope.

What we can handle

  • Plan and appliance-load review, routing, propane tank or natural gas connection planning.
  • Underground and aboveground piping, rough-in, appliance stub-outs, regulators, and final gas connections.
  • Gas diagrams and permit submittals, testing, inspection coordination, and completion records appropriate to the project.

How we coordinate

We identify the gas milestones and dependencies: utility readiness, trench access, framing, equipment delivery, finish work, and inspection access. Scope changes are reviewed before additional work proceeds.

What to send us

  1. Current plans, gas appliance schedule, jobsite address, and bid or construction deadline.
  2. Fuel source, intended tank/meter location, and requested rough-in and final dates.
  3. Site superintendent, billing/PO instructions, and subcontractor onboarding requirements.

Send revised plans and appliance substitutions before rough-in or ordering so the gas scope can be rechecked.

04Lateral Trades & RemodelersElectricians • plumbers • finishing trades

Keep your trade moving with the gas work covered.

Electricians, plumbers, remodelers, handyman services, tile installers, framers, roofers, and other trades can bring us in for the gas portion of a shared project.

What we can handle

  • Planned gas disconnection, capping, relocation, and reconnection within a defined work area.
  • Gas piping changes for kitchen remodels, appliance swaps, water-heater projects, and generator additions.
  • Coordination around walls, cabinets, tile, framing, roof work, and access needed for testing or inspection.

How we coordinate

We agree on the handoff points and site readiness with your team. Let us know before a pipe, vent, appliance connection, or gas equipment area will be disturbed or concealed.

What to send us

  1. Your trade, jobsite address, and a description of what is being changed.
  2. Photos of the gas equipment and work area, with appliance specifications if relevant.
  3. The date you need disconnection, rough-in, or reconnection and the onsite contact.

For finish-sensitive work, discuss access and restoration responsibilities before opening walls, floors, or cabinetry.

05Real Estate AgentsPre-sale • due diligence • post-sale

Get clear answers about the gas system.

Whether preparing a listing, responding to inspection findings, or helping a buyer after closing, a focused gas evaluation helps identify what needs attention.

What we can handle

  • Pre- and post-sale gas-system evaluations covering accessible gas piping, tank or meter components, and appliance connections.
  • Follow-up on gas-related home inspection findings, with testing included where agreed and appropriate.
  • Written findings and an estimate for identified gas repairs or improvements within our scope.

How we coordinate

We coordinate access with the authorized owner or representative and clarify the requested evaluation, available fuel supply, and transaction deadline before the visit.

What to send us

  1. Property address, inspection report or gas-related concern, and transaction deadline.
  2. Owner authorization, access instructions, and whether utilities are active.
  3. Tank ownership information if available and the report recipient’s contact details.

The evaluation covers the agreed gas scope and conditions observed; it is not a whole-home inspection or a guarantee of future system performance.

Manage the gas work across your properties.

07Commercial Kitchen OwnersRestaurants • bakeries • commissaries

Build the gas supply around your equipment lineup.

Opening, expanding, or rearranging a commercial kitchen? We coordinate the gas side of the project with the owner, general contractor, equipment supplier, and other responsible trades.

What we can handle

  • Gas-load review for ranges, ovens, fryers, griddles, water heating, and other specified equipment.
  • New or modified kitchen gas piping, regulators, shutoffs, and equipment connections.
  • Gas testing, permit and inspection coordination, and correction of gas-related deficiencies within the agreed scope.

How we coordinate

We identify supply and pressure needs, installation access, planned downtime, and dependencies on the utility and hood/fire-suppression providers. Those systems are coordinated with their responsible specialists.

What to send us

  1. Equipment list with model numbers, input ratings, fuel type, and required pressure.
  2. Kitchen layout, existing meter or tank details, and photos of connection points.
  3. Opening or shutdown schedule, site contact, and any review comments.

Replacing equipment with a larger model can affect supply capacity even when the connection appears to fit.

Prepare the gas side of your mobile kitchen.

Ask About My Project ↗
Do you work with propane and natural gas?

Yes. Tell us which fuel is available, or select “Not Sure” on the contact form. The equipment, supply, and installation approach will be reviewed for the project.

Can you work directly with my contractor or dealer?

Yes. Identify the contracting party, onsite contact, and the trades involved. We can coordinate the agreed gas scope around their work and your equipment delivery.

What do you need to prepare an estimate?

Start with the jobsite address, a description of the work, appliance model numbers, photos, and your timing. Plans, inspection comments, and existing tank or meter details are helpful when available. A site visit may be needed.

Do you handle permits and inspections?

Gas permit preparation and inspection coordination can be included where required and within the contracted scope. The relevant authority performs its inspections and makes approval decisions.

Can you inspect a system before or after a property sale?

Yes. Request a real estate gas evaluation and share the transaction deadline, inspection report, property access, and utility status. We confirm the evaluation and reporting scope before the visit.

Do you provide emergency or after-hours service?

No. GGS provides scheduled service. If you smell gas or suspect an active leak, leave the area and call your gas supplier or 911 from a safe location.

Can you coordinate recurring or multiple projects?

Tell us the anticipated locations, project types, timing, and onboarding requirements. We can discuss how to organize estimating, authorizations, site coordination, and documentation for the work.
