FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We've got answers. Below are some of the most common inquiries about our propane and natural gas services.

What areas do you service?
We serve North and Central Florida, including Marion, Orange, Sumter, Hernando, Levy, Alachua, Lake, and Citrus counties. We handle permitting across 38+ jurisdictions. Not sure if you're in our area? Call (877) 410-2195 and we'll confirm.
Is propane safe for home use?
Absolutely. Propane is safe when installed and maintained properly. We also offer safety inspections to ensure compliance with industry standards.
How long does a gas installation take?
Most residential installs are done within hours, usually less than a day, once permitting is in place. Larger projects (multi-appliance, generator hookups, community systems) run longer. We give you a realistic timeline in your written estimate.
Do you offer emergency repairs?
We do not offer emergency or after-hours service. If you smell gas, leave the area immediately and call your gas supplier or 911. For all other repair and service work, call us at (877) 410-2195 during business hours and we'll schedule you as soon as we can.
Can you convert my entire home to gas?
Yes. We specialize in whole-home gas conversions, providing efficient and safe energy solutions. A full conversion typically covers tank sizing, gas line routing, appliance hookups, and permit work, and can be done all at once or in stages.
How can I schedule a service?
You can schedule a service by calling us at (877) 410-2195 or filling out our contact form on the website. We respond within one business day.
