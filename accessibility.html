Accessibility & Assistance

Guardian Gas Solutions is working to make our website easier for everyone to use, including people with disabilities. Our technical accessibility target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA.

Need help or found a barrier?

Email Info@GoGuardianGas.com or call (877) 410-2195. You may contact us entirely by email. Include the page address, what you were trying to do, and your preferred contact method so we can help. You do not need to disclose a disability.

Our hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM Eastern. After-hours messages are returned the next business day. We do not provide emergency or after-hours service.

Using this website

Use the “Skip to main content” link with your keyboard, navigate controls with Tab, and activate buttons with Enter or Space. The mobile menu closes with Escape. You can enlarge text with your browser’s zoom controls. Use “Pause animations” in the footer to stop decorative motion; we also respect your device’s reduced-motion setting.

Continuing improvements

We have improved navigation, focus indicators, form labels and feedback, text contrast, and motion controls. Accessibility requires ongoing review as content changes. These improvements are not a certification of full WCAG conformance.

Linked third-party websites and downloadable documents, including propane safety PDFs, have not been fully evaluated for accessibility. If you encounter a barrier, contact us for help obtaining the information in an accessible format.
