Common Questions

FAQs

Common questions about residential propane and natural gas installation.

How long does residential gas line installation take?
Most single-appliance connections take a few hours, usually less than a day once the permit is in place. Propane tank, generator, and multi-appliance projects take longer. Your written estimate includes a realistic timeline.
Can you convert my entire home to gas?
Yes. We can plan a whole-home gas system or a phased conversion through the Guardian Blueprint. That covers supply review, tank or meter capacity, new piping and appliance connections.
Do you handle permits and inspections?
Yes. We prepare the applicable gas permits and coordinate required inspections for work in your agreed scope. Final approval rests with the local authority.
Do you install both propane and natural gas lines?
Yes, subject to property conditions and the availability of fuel. Not sure which you have? Choose “Not sure” on the estimate form, and we will check.
Can you run a gas line to my pool heater or outdoor kitchen?
Yes. Pool heater and outdoor kitchen gas line installation are among our most common projects. We size the line for your total household demand, so existing appliances keep full pressure.
Do you provide emergency gas leak service?
No. Guardian Gas Solutions provides scheduled service, not emergency or after-hours response. If you suspect a gas leak, leave the area and call your gas supplier or 911 from a safe location.
